## 2. Positioning

MEMECYCLE is a historical intelligence and evidence archive for Solana tokens. It helps people inspect recorded market activity, token history, available provenance and lifecycle observations.

Use careful language:
- Say "recorded market history", "observed market state", "available evidence" and "research lead".
- Do not call a token abandoned unless there is independently verified evidence and a documented review.
- Do not imply that low liquidity, missing data or a quiet pool proves a project is dead.
- Do not promise recovery, revival, returns or investment outcomes.
- Clearly distinguish observed facts, third-party data and unverified leads.

## 5. Weekly publishing rhythm

A manageable starting cadence that costs nothing:

- Monday: one research insight or data-quality lesson.
- Wednesday: one product/build update with a screenshot or public page link.
- Friday: one token-history case study, only where the available evidence supports it.
- Weekend: answer relevant community questions and collect feedback.

Repurpose the same core research for X and LinkedIn, but adapt the wording to each community. Do not post the same promotional message repeatedly in Reddit, Telegram or Discord.

## 6. Manual publishing steps

For each post:

1. Open the relevant native social app or website and sign in to the account you already use.
2. Copy one post from this file into the native composer.
3. Check that the MEMECYCLE link opens: https://memecycle.vercel.app
4. Add a genuine product screenshot or a link to a specific public token page when it adds evidence. Never fabricate metrics or use a screenshot that implies unsupported functionality.
5. Read the platform/community rules. On Reddit, Telegram and Discord, contribute to the discussion first and only share links where permitted.
6. Publish manually.
7. Record the date, platform, post link, impressions, meaningful replies, site visits and sign-ups in a simple free spreadsheet.
